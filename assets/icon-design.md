Strong simple silhouette, generous negative space, precise balanced geometry. Refined Apple-style visual craft: luminous porcelain-white symbol with very subtle cool glass depth, on a full-bleed near-black graphite square with a restrained soft charcoal gradient. A tiny hint of cool blue in the glass edge is acceptable, but primarily monochrome. The icon artwork fills the square canvas, no surrounding presentation background, no mockup, no extra tile placed on a larger background, no external drop shadow. Do not include an Apple logo, letters, words, Chinese characters, CC lettering, product name, Loon name, stars, sparkles, robot, rainbow gradient or watermark. No beige or cream. Deliver a single polished centered icon, not a contact sheet. Intended final use is a small plugin-list icon.
